AI Interview Kickstart

Frontend redesign

Three screens, every state, light and dark. Neutral zinc palette, one indigo accent, Geist for type. Everything below maps onto shadcn/ui tokens and Tailwind v4 — no new libraries.

01 · One accent

Indigo means exactly two things: “act here” (primary button, focus ring) and “someone is speaking”. Everything else is grey. Red is reserved for ending the call and errors.

02 · Motion carries meaning

The only continuous motion is the voice halo tracking audio level, plus spinners while waiting. Colour/opacity changes run 150 ms ease-out. With reduced motion, the halo stops scaling and becomes a static on/off ring.

03 · Errors live in the layout

Every failure is shown inline where it happened, with the fix and a retry. Sonner toasts are optional echoes, never the only signal. Status changes go through one aria-live region per screen.
